Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

Insane Game is a personal content project. The "Insane Game" application is a private tool used only by the channel owner to publish Insane Game videos.

What the application accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (scope youtube.upload) to the owner's own channel. It does not read, modify or delete other content.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's own professional account.

What we collect

The application does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from viewers or any other user. Access tokens belonging to the owner are stored encrypted in the owner's private infrastructure and are used only to publish videos.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or from Instagram's settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on github.com/Pergoat.
